CreatorFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

CreatorFlow is a personal content creation and publishing workflow.

Information We Process

CreatorFlow may process information required to connect with third-party platforms, including account authorization data and content selected for upload or publishing.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the requested content creation, upload, and publishing functionality.

Third-Party Services

CreatorFlow may interact with third-party services. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

CreatorFlow does not sell personal information. Information is shared only when necessary to provide the requested functionality through connected third-party services.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary for the operation of CreatorFlow or as required by applicable services or law.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the CreatorFlow operator.
